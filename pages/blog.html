My goals for this year

My mission for this year is when I start working in a team, I will try to help the team socialize more with each other to help the work go smoother and issueless. I will also start organizing my time efficiently so that I don't overwork anymore and have more energy to help with the task in my team. I will keep organizing it for 66 days, because that's the time the human mind needs to learn a new habit. Another thing about my work in a team is that every time the team has problems or needs help, I will be there for them and support them with everything I can, so that they become friendlier with each other and help the flow of the work run more smoothly. By doing this goal for 66 days and until my team's project is over or until the problem is resolved, I will improve myself as a person too.
