Why IT?

IT (Information Technology) is a field that fascinates me for several reasons. Firstly, it is constantly evolving, which means there is always something new to learn and explore. This dynamic nature keeps the field exciting and challenging.

Secondly, IT has a significant impact on society. From improving communication and collaboration to driving, innovation in various industries, the role of IT professionals is crucial. I find it rewarding to be part of this transformative field.

Lastly, the problem-solving aspect of IT appeals to me. Whether it's debugging code or designing efficient systems, I enjoy the process of identifying challenges and developing creative solutions.

What motivates me about learning ICT is that I can make different apps and programs for different purposes that help people. Also, it can improve my critical thinking and expand my programming skills. The reason why I was motivated in learning more about ICT and coding is that when I was 3 years old, I downloaded my first game, and I was so impressed that it ignited the fire in me that made me want to learn more about how games and apps work, and make some of my own. How it motivates me is that whenever I get stuck at a certain problem I don't give up easily, I try to find different solutions which stimulates my mind into thinking logically and critically, and it also improves my determination.
